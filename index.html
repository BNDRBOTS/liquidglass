About

Sculpted by fire, inspired by red rock.

Terra Sedona is a contemporary glass gallery and wellness boutique located in the heart of Sedona's uptown arts district. Each piece in our collection is hand-crafted by local and visiting artists, drawing inspiration from the region's iconic red rock formations, vortex energy, and high desert light.

From luminous blown glass sculptures to meditative crystal vessels, our curated selection bridges the worlds of fine art, functional design, and spiritual practice.
